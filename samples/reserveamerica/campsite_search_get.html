Alerts and Important Information

September 26, 2026 - Boat rentals are limited due to the low lake level.

August 14, 2026 - Boat rentals will not be available when the heat index is above 100° F. Contact the park with questions.

Grills and deep fryers are prohibited in portions of the day-use area, including the designated swim area/bath house/parking lot, the park store area, and the area directly adjacent to the playground.

Diving or swimming directly around the fishing piers and boat ramp is prohibited. Divers must check-in and out at the park headquarters, during business hours.

If your plans change, please modify or cancel your reservation as soon as possible to allow someone else to enjoy the park.

DAY USE FEE:
$6 per day, per person 13 and older.

OFFICE AND GATE INFORMATION:
Day Use hours are 8am to 10pm.

Park office Hours are 8:30am-4:30pm daily. Seasonally the park stays open later on Friday through Sunday.

An honor box at headquarters is used to collect camping and day use fees when the office is closed.

Park Store Open year round; Memorial Day Weekend through Labor Day Open daily. Tuesday following Labor Day through Friday before Memorial Day, Park Store is closed Tuesday and Wednesday.

SWIMMING:
Swimming is prohibited Sunset to Sunrise.

FIREWOOD:
Firewood is available at the park.

PARK PHONE NUMBER: 903-597-5338

Notes

Campers will receive 4 digit gate code for after hours entry.

If arriving after hours, a camping reservation listing will be posted at the Headquarters Late Arrival Board. Campers with reservations should proceed to your reserved campsite. All after-hours customers without a prior camping reservation must use the green self-pay station located at Headquarters. Fill out the envelope and make proper payment before proceeding to an available site labeled with a Green A on the site number post. Adhere the top white sheet to the left side of your windshield. All overnight customers arriving after hours must register at the Park Headquarters between 8:40 am and 10:00 am the next morning.

Cabin and Shelter Reservations:
If arriving after hours, you must call the Park Headquarters at 903-597-5338 to let them know you will be a late arrival before 3:00 pm daily, a reservation listing with your cabin assignments will be posted at the Headquarters Late Arrival Board. Cabins will be left unlocked. All customers arriving after hours must register at the Park Headquarters between 8:40 am and 10:00 am the next morning to register and receive your key. .

Fees and Cancellation Policies
Special Policy Applicable to this Facility
No-shows:
If you do not check in at the park by noon of the day after your scheduled arrival date, we will cancel the rest of your reservation. Your entrance fees will be refunded, but all other fees will be forfeited.

If you think you will arrive after the noon cutoff, call the park or the Customer Service Center to avoid cancellation.
