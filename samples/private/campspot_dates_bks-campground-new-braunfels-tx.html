About

Nestled along the scenic banks of the Guadalupe River, BK’s Campground offers an authentic back-to-basics escape for those who want to experience the true beauty of the Texas Hill Country. Located on the iconic River Road between the second and third crossings, this destination specializes in primitive waters-edge camping, providing a peaceful alternative to the crowded resorts while keeping guests right in the heart of the action. Whether visitors are pitching a tent under a canopy of trees, launching a tube for a day on the river, or enjoying a quiet evening by the fire, the park’s direct river access and laid-back atmosphere make it the ultimate home base for outdoor enthusiasts. Call today to reserve your primitive riverfront site and reclaim your connection with nature at BK’s Campground.
